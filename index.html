[ Method ]

One active build at a time. A written scope, a fixed fee, and progress you can see every week, with the person doing the work on the other end of every message.

  1. 01

    Find the useful problem

    What is working, what is in the way, and what would make a real difference.

  2. 02

    Agree on the shape

    A written scope, one fixed number and a realistic schedule before work begins.

  3. 03

    Build with a clear view

    Design and development in one pair of hands, shared weekly, with focused feedback.

  4. 04

    Launch with confidence

    Tested, checked and handed over with a recorded walkthrough. Care is there if you want it.
